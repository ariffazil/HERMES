## Technique 1: Box Breathing (Navy SEAL Method)

**Use:** Waktu floating P/L, bila tengah anxious tengok chart.

```
Tarik nafas hidung — 4 saat
Tahan — 4 saat
Hembus mulut — 4 saat
Tahan — 4 saat
```

**Ulang 5 kali.** Reset nervous system. Turunkan heart rate. Bila otak laju, box breathing slowkan balik.

**Why it works:** Equal-duration phases engage parasympathetic nervous system. Heart rate variability improves. Decision quality returns.

---

## Technique 2: Physiological Sigh (Andrew Huberman)

**Use:** Selepas SL trigger. Bila nak revenge trade. Quick reset.

```
Tarik nafas dalam-dalam hidung
Lepas tu satu lagi sedutan pendek (double inhale)
Pastu hembus panjang-panjang mulut
```

**Buat 3 kali je.** Lepas tu rasa enteng — macam reset button untuk emosi.

**Why it works:** Double inhale reinflates collapsed alveoli, long exhale activates vagus nerve. Fastest way to down-regulate stress response.

## Technique 3: Pre-Entry Breath Check

**Use:** Sebelum tekan BUY/SELL. Gate terakhir.

```
Tarik nafas 10 saat
Hembus lambat-lambat
Tanya diri:
  - "Aku tenang ke sekarang?"
  - "Ini calculated risk, atau impulse?"

Kalau lepas hembus still nak entry → masuk
Kalau rasa ragu → tutup app. Esok je.
```

---

## Technique 4: 4-7-8 Breathing (Dr. Andrew Weil)

**Use:** General chill. Malam sebelum tidur. Untuk dapatkan deep sleep.

```
Tarik nafas hidung — 4 saat
Tahan — 7 saat
Hembus mulut — 8 saat (bunyi whoosh)
```

**Ulang 4 kali.** Best dilakukan baring. Combine dengan Hypnos untuk maximum sleep quality.

---

## Technique 5: Wim Hof Style (Energy Boost)

**Use:** Waktu brain fog tapi kena trade (demo only — x boleh live trade).

```
30 deep breaths (tarik power, hembus relax)
Last exhale — TAHAN (jangan bernafas)
Bila urge nafas — tarik dalam, tahan 15 saat
Repeat 3 rounds
```

**⚠️ ONLY for demo trading. Never live trade after Wim Hof — altered state impairs risk judgment.**
