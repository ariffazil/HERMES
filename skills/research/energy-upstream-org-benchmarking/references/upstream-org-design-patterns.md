# Upstream Org Design Patterns — Session Research (2026-07-31)

## IOCs — Detailed Org Structures

### ExxonMobil
- **Divisions**: Upstream, Product Solutions (refining+chemicals merged 2022), Low Carbon Solutions, Global Projects
- **Upstream organised as 5 portfolio businesses**: Unconventional, Conventional, Deepwater, Heavy Oil, LNG
- **62K employees**, $340B revenue (FY2024)
- **Rigid internal hierarchy** — promote-from-within culture
- **Pioneer Natural Resources acquisition (2024)**: added 2,000 Permian-focused employees, independent-producer culture to absorb
- **JVs in 40+ countries** create complex co-governance structures
- **Source**: corporate.exxonmobil.com, orgbrief.com

### Shell (2026)
- **3 primary business areas**: Integrated Gas, Upstream, Downstream/Renewables & Energy Solutions
- **Trading & Supply elevated** in 2025-26 restructuring
- **Projects & Technology dissolved Jan 2026** — absorbed into existing business segments
- **Simplified from dual-listed** (UK/Netherlands) to single UK HQ in 2022
- **LNG business** is world's largest, carries significant org weight
- **Source**: orgbrief.com, stocktitan.net, fuelsandlubes.com

### BP (2026)
- **Major reversal**: Meg O'Neill (CEO Apr 2026) reverting to 2-unit structure: Upstream, Downstream
- **Bernard Looney's 2020 reorg** had split into 3 units (Gas & Low Carbon, Oil Production & Ops, Customers & Products)
- **Elliott Management (5% stake)** forced the reversal — investors punished the green push
- **Fifth CEO since 2020** — significant instability at top
- **Source**: hydrocarbonprocessing.com

### Chevron
- Similar divisional model to ExxonMobil
- Upstream, Downstream & Chemicals, Midstream
- **341 main executives** tracked on theofficialboard.com
- Lean corporate centre

## NOCs — Detailed Org Structures

### Saudi Aramco (2026)
- **81.48% government-owned** (post-IPO, post-secondary offerings to PIF and public)
- **Board** → **President & CEO (Amin Nasser)** → **Upstream President**, **Downstream President**, **EVP & CFO**, plus EVPs for HR, Technical Services, Legal, Strategy, Technology
- **20+ divisions**, each led by SVP
- **2020 downstream reorg**: launched 4 commercial BUs: fuels, chemicals, power, pipelines/distribution/terminals
- **Highly centralised** — global functions controlled from Dhahran
- **Bureaucratic by design** — stability over speed
- **Source**: ARAMCO 2025 Annual Report (governance PDF, March 2026)

### PETRONAS
- **4 segments**: Upstream, Gas & Energy, Downstream, Project Delivery & Technology
- **Upstream arm**: PETRONAS Carigali (operator, 60% of Malaysian assets) + PMU (PSC regulator)
- **Unique dual role** among NOCs — both operator and regulator
- **Activity Outlook 2026-2028** published Jan 2026 — 3-year visibility for OGSE
- **Classified as "Entrepreneurial NOC"** — operates commercially despite full state ownership
- **Source**: petronas.com, cgaa.org, businesstoday.com.my

## Upstream Discipline Roles (from Petropt 2026)

| Role | Core Question | Tools/Deliverables |
|---|---|---|
| **Geologist** | Where is the rock? Depositional environment, structure, faults? | Maps, cross-sections, depositional models |
| **Geophysicist** | What does seismic tell us? | Seismic interpretation, attributes, DHI |
| **Petrophysicist** | Porosity, saturation, lithology? | Log analysis, core integration, net pay |
| **Reservoir Engineer** | How do fluids move? Recovery factor? | Simulation, forecasts, development plans |
| **Drilling Engineer** | How to get to TD safely? | Well path, casing, mud program, rig selection |
| **Completions Engineer** | How to make well productive? | Perf strategy, frac design, sand control |
| **Production Engineer** | How to keep well producing? | Artificial lift, surveillance, chemical programs |
| **Facilities Engineer** | Surface systems? | Separators, compressors, pipelines, water handling |
| **Asset Manager** | Integrate all into a plan. Owns P&L. | Budget, sequencing, capital allocation |
| **Reserves Coordinator** | SEC/PRMS compliance? | Documentation, third-party audit, classification |

## The Three-Tier Gatekeeping Model (ModalPoint 2026)

The real decision-making structure in any major energy company:

1. **Technical Authority** (SME/Lead Engineer) — vetoes anything that creates data silos, doesn't integrate with Digital Twin
2. **Procurement Professional** — Total Cost of Ownership, Integration Maturity (not lowest bid)
3. **ESG/Sustainability Officer** — every CAPEX must pass carbon intensity filter

## 2026 Trends Summary

1. **Back to basics**: BP reversing green reorg, Shell dissolving P&T, everyone simplifying
2. **Low Carbon becomes a division**: Not a sideline anymore
3. **Asset Team empowerment**: Decentralised, basin-level P&L ownership
4. **Digital Twin**: IT/OT convergence teams have procurement veto power
5. **ROIC over production**: Breakeven cost per barrel is the new religion
6. **Entrepreneurial NOCs behave like IOCs**: PETRONAS, Equinor, Petrobras mirror IOC structures
