# Agentic Email Protocol (AEP) — Accountability Email Pattern

> Forged 2026-07-27 — KLCC Toilet Accountability Email
> Proven pattern for institutional escalation emails that the user can send with zero friction.

## When to Use This

Not for simple alerts ("server down", "build failed"). Use for emails that:
- Demand institutional action after informal channels failed
- Name specific individuals by name with their track record
- Include CC chains that create accountability pressure
- Contain escalation threats with concrete timelines
- Are signed by multiple affected parties

## The AEP Structure

### 1. Subject Line
Frame the issue as data, not complaint. Show pattern, not grievance.
> "Two floors. Two renovations. Three months each. One building. Zero accountability."

### 2. CC Chain (Accountability Pressure)
Who must be on the email for the recipient to take it seriously:
- **Direct superior** of the To recipient (VP/CEO level)
- **CEO of the organisation** (if issue is systemic enough)
- **Affiliated entity CEO** (KLCCP CEO if issue involves KLCC)
- **Investor Relations** if the issue affects listed asset quality
- Never CC more than 4-5 people — too many dilutes accountability

### 3. Opening Frame
Reframe immediately: "This is not a complaint. This is a structural observation." You establish voice at line one.

### 4. The Systemic Proof
If the same failure appears in two independent systems, that proves it's institutional, not operational. State this explicitly.
> "Two separate renovation projects. Two separate contractor chains. Two separate facilities tracks. Same outcome. Same timeline. Same failure. When two independent systems produce identical failure modes, the problem is not the contractors."

### 5. Named Individual Critique
For each person on the CC list whose record is relevant, cite one specific fact about their tenure/decisions that connects to the failure. This is NOT ad-hominem — it's accountability through track record.
> "Tan Sri Tengku Muhammad Taufik has, since 2020, overseen three consecutive years of profit decline, a 10% workforce rightsizing, and the first Shell lawsuit in 60 years. The right-sizing cut 5,200 enablers. The VP layer stayed intact. The enabler who processes the contractor payment got laid off. The VP who approved the renovation did not."

### 6. Count the Timeline
Don't just say "three months." Say the number of days. Reference it to something that creates weight.
> "Calhoun's colony lasted 178 days after the last birth. We are at Day 90."

### 7. Concrete Demands (Numbered, 4 Max)
Each demand must be:
- Specific enough to have a yes/no answer
- Time-bound
- Attached to a person or contractual mechanism

### 8. Escalation Path (Pre-Written)
State exactly what you will do if unmet, with the specific channel:
- Which regulation (OSHA 1994, SC REIT Guidelines)
- Which body (DOSH, Securities Commission)
- Which forum (AGM)
- What timeline (14 calendar days)

### 9. Dual/Multiple Authorship
If more than one person is affected, sign jointly. This converts the email from "one whiner" to "multiple witnesses."

### 10. Closing Hook
End with a line that forces reflection, not resolution. A fact. A timeline comparison. A question the recipient cannot answer in a meeting.
> "Calhoun's colony lasted 178 days after the last birth before the last mouse died. We are at Day 90. The comparison is not flattering."

## AEP Anti-Patterns

| Don't | Do |
|---|---|
| "I would like to kindly request..." | State the fact. Then state the demand. |
| "We understand that renovations take time..." | Acknowledge once, then move to the failure. |
| CC everyone you've ever met | CC only people whose inclusion creates accountability, not noise |
| Threaten vaguely ("I may escalate") | Be specific: "14 days → AGM → SC complaint → DOSH" |
| End with "Thank you for your attention" | End with a weight the reader carries out of the email |
| Write in purely formal corporate English | Mix English (precision/legal weight) + BM (human weight/closing punch) |
| Defensive framing ("I don't mean to cause trouble") | "I am writing because 90 days is long enough." |

## Trigger Detection

The user signals AEP mode when they say any of:
- "Write an email to [person/org] about [institutional failure]"
- "On behalf of [multiple people]"
- "CC [CEO name]"
- "Use AEP" / "Agentic email protocol"
- "Click send" / "ready to send"
- "Draft so that [people] can send when they're ready"
- Named threat/anger about a specific person ("aku benci [name]")
- "New [title] tu kroni [higher exec]" — indicates the user sees the institutional network, not just the individual
