# Malaysia Medical Negligence — Key Facts for Advisory

## Government Hospital Pricing (HKL / Hospital Kerajaan)

**CRITICAL:** Government hospital costs are DRAMATICALLY lower than private.

| Procedure | Private estimate | Gov hospital (HKL) |
|---|---|---|
| ERCP | RM 15,000 – 25,000 | RM 500 – 2,000 |
| Perforation repair surgery | RM 40,000+ | RM 2,000 – 5,000 |
| ICU per day | RM 3,000 – 5,000 | RM 500 – 1,500 |
| Emergency laparotomy | RM 30,000 – 50,000 | RM 2,000 – 5,000 |
| Re-admission | RM 15,000+ | RM 1,000 – 3,000 |
| **Total typical negligence case** | **RM 80,000 – 150,000** | **RM 5,000 – 25,000** |

**Never assume private hospital pricing for government hospital cases.**
The total quantum may be small enough that litigation cost exceeds recovery.

## ERCP Complication ≠ Negligence

ERCP (Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography) perforation is a
**known complication**, not automatically negligence:

- Perforation rate: **0.3% – 5%** depending on procedure complexity
- If perforation occurs within known complication rates AND the doctor
  followed standard protocol → **NOT negligence, it is an inherent risk**
- Negligence requires proving the doctor deviated from **standard of care**

## What Constitutes Negligence (Malaysia — Bolam Test)

Malaysia uses a modified **Bolam test**:
1. Did the doctor act in accordance with a **responsible body of medical
   opinion** within that specialty?
2. Is there a **logical basis** for that opinion?
3. The opinion must be **defensible** — not just popular

**If even ONE qualified expert says "doctor acted reasonably" → case fails.**

## Evidence Requirements for Court

| What holds up | What does not |
|---|---|
| Formal medico-legal report from registered specialist | Casual comment from doctor friend |
| Written statement under oath | AI analysis / opinion |
| Hospital protocol documentation showing violation | Assumption based on outcome |
| Nursing notes showing ignored warning signs | "It should not have happened" |

## Contingency Fee Reality (Malaysia)

- Typical: **20 – 30%** of settlement / award
- PI (personal injury) lawyers often take medical negligence on contingency
- BUT: case must have realistic quantum to attract contingency
- If gov hospital total = RM 10,000 – 25,000, very few lawyers will take it
  on contingency (their fee exceeds realistic recovery)

## Litigation Timeline (Malaysia)

- Medical negligence case: **3 – 7 years** typical
- Pre-trial: 1 – 2 years (discovery, expert reports)
- Trial: 1 – 3 years (delays common)
- Appeal: 1 – 2 years additional
- Settlement often offered pre-trial at **30 – 50%** of claimed quantum
