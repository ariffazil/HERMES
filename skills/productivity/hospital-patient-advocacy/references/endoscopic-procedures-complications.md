# Endoscopic Procedure Post-Complication Knowledge

## EUS (Endoscopic Ultrasonography)
- Combines gastroscopy with ultrasound probe
- Visualises CBD, pancreas, lymph nodes, ampulla
- Perforation rate: 0.03-0.1%
- DIAGNOSTIC only — no duct entry
- Patient may refer to it simply as "endoscopy" or "endos"
- Procedure report explicitly says "Endoscopic Ultrasonography" or "EUS"

## ERCP
- Therapeutic — enters bile/pancreatic duct
- Uses wire + contrast + X-ray
- Can perform sphincterotomy, stone extraction, stent placement
- Perforation rate: 0.3-0.6%
- Patient may also call this "endoscopy"

## Post-Procedure Observation Protocol (standard of care)

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 0-2h post-procedure | Observe in recovery bay. Check vitals q15-30min. Assess pain level. |
| 2-4h post-procedure | Oral intake trial. Monitor for abdominal distension, nausea, fever. |
| 4-6h post-procedure | If stable and pain-free, discharge with verbal + written instructions. |
| Any time | Pain unresponsive to analgesia, distension, fever, rigors → suspect perforation. Immediate CT abdomen / erect CXR. |

## Red flags for perforation
- Abdominal pain disproportionate to expected post-procedure discomfort
- Pain radiating to shoulder (referred from diaphragm irritation)
- Abdominal distension / bloating
- Nausea/vomiting
- Fever / rigors
- Inability to pass flatus
- Tachycardia, hypotension (late sign — may indicate sepsis/shock)

## Standard discharge criteria (should be met)
- Pain well-controlled on oral analgesia
- Tolerating oral fluids
- Normal vital signs
- No abdominal distension
- Passed flatus
- Verbal discharge instructions given to patient/guardian

## Key abbreviations
- CBD: Common Bile Duct
- EUS: Endoscopic Ultrasonography
- ERCP: Endoscopic Retrograde Cholangiopancreatography
- OGDS: Oesophago-Gastro-DuodenoScopy (gastroscopy)
- MRCP: Magnetic Resonance Cholangiopancreatography
- HCU: High Care Unit
- NPO: Nil By Mouth
