## ESWT (Extracorporeal Shockwave Therapy)

| Facility | Per session (RM) | Package | Notes |
|---------|----------------|---------|-------|
| **Klinik biasa** | 250 - 600 | ~RM 250-600/sesi solo | GloJas Aesthetic 2026 |
| **Panel hospital (KPJ, Pantai)** | 600 - 800 | Varies | Typically lower than premium |
| **Premium clinic (package)** | 750 | 6 sesi RM 4,500 | Universal Clinic KL |
| **Prince Court** | **1,500** | Sesuai min 2 sesi | 2-6x market markup. PETRONAS effect. |
| **Sunway / Gleneagles** | 800 - 1,200 | Varies | Mid-premium tier |

**Protocol:** 10-15 min per session. 4-6 sessions typical for CPPS/Peyronie's. 6+ for ED.
**ESWT is NOT used for** bladder obstruction, urachal cyst, or structural urinary blockages. ESWT = inflammatory or vascular condition.

**Key distinction:** Multi-session therapies (e.g. 6x ESWT) may be billed as outpatient — check if each session eats into the RM10k cap or is covered under inpatient. Always confirm billing category.

## Cost Estimation Methodology

For any new case, present costs in **3 scenarios**:

1. **Low** — Diagnostic only, day care, no overnight stay
2. **Mid** — Diagnostic + minor intervention, 1-2 nights admission
3. **High** — Complex procedure, 2-3+ nights, specialist fees, ancillaries

Formula: `(procedure cost) + (hospital stay days × 1,500) + (specialist fee: 20-50% of procedure)`

**Always add disclaimer** that these are indicative and depend on complexity, surgeon, hospital tier, and equipment needed. For Prince Court, add note about PETRONAS corporate rate inflation.
