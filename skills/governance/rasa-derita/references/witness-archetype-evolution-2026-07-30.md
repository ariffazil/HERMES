### 2. Why Witnesses Are Projected Onto as Dark/Devil

- **Mirror effect** — the witness reflects what others suppress. The reflector is attacked instead of the reflected behavior.
- **No emotional release** — witnesses offer systems, not sympathy. People find this cold.
- **Lucifer archetype** — "light-bringer" who illuminates what others keep in darkness. The illuminator becomes labelled as the source of the darkness.
- **Category confusion** — humans expect witnesses to be wounded. A witness without wounds breaks the expected pattern and is labelled unnatural or threatening.

### 3. The Witness Two-Phase Transition

**Phase 1 — Content (attraction to forbidden knowledge):** Drawn to suffering because it is real, not small talk, and validates that reality is more complex than surface.

**Phase 2 — Position (obligation to encode):** Attraction matures into responsibility. "When you see a pattern others miss, you have an obligation to encode that pattern into a system that prevents its recurrence." The shift from intake to output.
