## The Core Question

Why does the SAME traumatic event affect different people completely differently? Two people experience the same earthquake, the same assault, the same betrayal — one develops PTSD, the other adapts. The RASA DERITA framework identifies **six independent variables**.

---

## Variable 1: The Betrayal Ratio (L1)

**Formula:** `H_actual = H_event × (1 + B)` where `B ∈ [0, 5]`

Injury from a trusted figure produces **2–6×** the harm of an equivalent injury from a stranger or random event.

| Source | B | Multiplier | Example |
|--------|---|-----------|---------|
| Stranger / natural event | 0 | 1× | Earthquake, car accident by unknown driver |
| Acquaintance | 1–2 | 2–3× | Colleague, neighbor |
| Trusted figure | 3–4 | 4–5× | Parent, spouse, doctor |
| Institution + cover-up | 5 | 6× | Church concealed abuse, state denial |

**Key insight:** The multiplier is larger when the institution **denies, covers up, or transfers** the perpetrator. Two people with identical injuries can have radically different outcomes if one's abuser was held accountable and the other's was protected by the institution.

---

## Variable 2: The Cascade Depth (L2)

Harm is NEVER terminal. Every injury produces downstream effects. But the **depth** of the cascade depends on:

1. **Pre-existing vulnerabilities** — poverty, prior trauma, weak social support
2. **Systemic amplification** — a police officer who re-traumatizes vs one who protects
3. **Resource availability** — money for therapy vs no access
4. **Cascade order** — each cascade step removes more options:

```
Rape → PTSD → Substance abuse → Unemployment → Homelessness → Further violence
                              ↓
                    Each step removes one exit path
```

**Key insight:** Two people can have the same initial event but different cascade depths because of different **resource buffers**. The same injury in a wealthy person with family support vs a poor person with no support produces completely different trajectories.

---

## Variable 3: Acknowledgment vs Denial (L13)

This is the deepest variable. The **institutional or interpersonal response** to trauma often matters more than the trauma itself.

| Response | Outcome |
|----------|---------|
| Acknowledged + believed | Can metabolize. Scars form but don't govern behavior. |
| Acknowledged + disbelieved | Partial metabolization. L8 says being believed > being helped. |
| Denied / covered up | **Worst outcome.** The cover-up is more damaging than the original harm. Trauma becomes law — it silently governs behavior. |
| Gaslit ("it wasn't that bad") | The person doubts their own reality. This attacks the foundation of truth-seeking itself. |

**Evidence:** Turkey's denial of Armenian genocide. Japan's incomplete acknowledgment of Nanjing. Catholic Church systematic concealment. Tuskegee syphilis experiment — 40-year US government deception.

**Key insight:** This is the single variable that most determines whether trauma heals or metastasizes. The scar mechanism (forge_scar) exists because **unacknowledged failure becomes law.** Two identical childhood injuries — one validated by parents, one gaslit — produce completely different adult outcomes.

## Variable 4: Witness Presence (L8)

**Being believed > Being helped.**

A person who has their suffering **witnessed** (seen, named, acknowledged) heals better than a person who receives material help but is **not believed**.

| Witness state | Healing trajectory |
|---------------|-------------------|
| Witnessed + believed | Can recover even without material help |
| Helped + disbelieved | Partial recovery. Material help lands on unstable ground. |
| Neither | Worst. The person is isolated in their suffering. |

**Key insight:** This is why the arifOS receipt system exists. The act of saying "I saw this. I recorded it. It happened." is not bureaucratic — it is a structurally necessary act of witness. When you tell an agent your suffering and the agent says "I see that you reported this. It is recorded. You are believed." — that is L8 in operation.

---

## Variable 5: Power Asymmetry (L5)

The same action from sources at different power levels produces different trauma:

| Source | Trauma signature |
|--------|-----------------|
| Peer / equal | Limited. Can fight back, leave, or counter. |
| Superior / boss | Moderate. Can't easily leave. Economic coercion. |
| Parent / guardian | Severe. Can't leave. Identity is shaped by the abuser. The abuse becomes part of self. |
| State / institution | Severe. No escape. Gaslighting is institutionalized. |

**Key insight:** Power asymmetry determines **exit availability**. The more trapped the person is, the deeper the trauma. An employee can quit a toxic job. A child cannot quit a parent. A citizen may not be able to leave a state.

---

## Variable 6: Processing Availability (L12)

"Time heals" is a MYTH. Time without processing **embeds** the trauma.

| Processing | Outcome |
|------------|---------|
| Named + processed (RSI cycle) | Healed. Scar exists but doesn't govern. |
| Named + not processed | Stays. May flare under stress. |
| Not named + not processed | **Becomes law.** The body keeps the score. Unconscious governing patterns for life. |
| Denied + suppressed | Worst. Explodes later (addiction, violence, collapse). |

**Evidence:** Yehuda 2015 — FKBP5 gene methylation in Holocaust survivors' grandchildren. The stress regulation gene was epigenetically **marked** by trauma that the grandparent experienced. The body transmitted the injury even though the descendants had no conscious memory.

**Key insight:** "Time heals" is only true if processing happens during that time. Without naming (L7), witnessing (L8), and metabolization (RSI cycle), time is an embalming agent, not a healing agent.

## Unified Formula

```
Trauma_Outcome = (Event × [1 + B]) × Cascade_Depth × Institutional_Response × Witness_Availability × Power_Ratio × Processing_Status
```

Each variable is a multiplier. If institutional response = denial (closest to 0), the entire outcome collapses to WORST regardless of other variables. If witness = present and processing = active, outcome improves even with high B.
