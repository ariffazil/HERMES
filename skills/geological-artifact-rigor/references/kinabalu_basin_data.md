## Tectonic Event Table

| Event | Age | Key Feature | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sarawak Orogeny | ~40 Ma (Late Eocene) | Folding/thrusting of basement + Paleogene. Rajang Group uplift. Unconformity within Paleogene turbidites | Balaguru 2009 |
| Sabah Orogeny | 22-20 Ma (E. Miocene) | Dangerous Grounds collision. Mélanges. **Base Miocene Unconformity (BMU)**. Deep marine → shallow deltaic transition | Balaguru 2009; Hutchison 1996 |
| Cagayan Arc collision | 15.5 Ma (M. Miocene) | Arc-continent collision. **Mid-Miocene Unconformity (MMU) = Deep Regional Unconformity (DRU)**. End of extension, inversion begins | Balaguru 2009 |
| Kinabalu emplacement | 10-8 Ma (L. Miocene) | Granite intrusion. **Shallow Regional Unconformity (SRU, 8.6 Ma)**. Sediment supply surge. Baram/Kinabatangan delta progradation | Balaguru 2009 |
| Meliau Orogeny | L. Pliocene–Recent | NW-SE strike-slip transpression. Final structural configuration. "Circular basins" dissection. Ongoing deformation | Balaguru et al. 2003 |

## Petroleum System

| Element | Description | Quality |
|---|---|---|
| Source rock | Oligocene–L. Miocene deep marine shales (Type II/III) | MODERATE–GOOD |
| Reservoir | M. Miocene turbidite sandstones (post-MMU), deeper Oligocene syn-rift clastics | GOOD (proven) |
| Seal | Intraformational shales, MMU regional seal | GOOD |
| Trap | Anticlinal closures in fold-thrust belt, stratigraphic pinchouts | PROVEN |
| Migration | Vertical along faults, lateral along carrier beds | EFFICIENT |
| Key risk | Deeper charge access — gas-dominant perception challenged by Well A oil discovery | EMERGING |

## Production Status (PETRONAS MPM 2025)

- One of 3 producing basins in Malaysia (with Malay & Sarawak)
- Recent discoveries: **Zoisit Deep-1** (gas/oil deeper pool)
- **Well A**: *OIL in deeper intervals beneath known gas* — paradigm shift
- **Well B**: reprocessed seismic → deeper play extension
- Adjacent Layang-Layang Basin: **Megah-1** (Malaysia's largest recent oil find in Oligocene carbonate)

## Key Uncertainties (UNKNOWN / DEBATED)

1. Three competing models for NE Borneo Cenozoic extension remain unresolved
2. Deeper oil charge mechanism (separate kitchen? phase segregation? multi-episode charge?)
3. Extent of Oligocene syn-rift plays beneath the post-MMU fold belt
4. Exact PSC block boundaries (confidential until data room)
