# EasyParcel / Courier Claim Process Reference

Source: EasyParcel Help Centre (helpcentre-my.easyparcel.com) — verified live 2026-07-30.

## Insurance Tiers

### 1. Basic Insurance (FREE — auto included)

| Courier | Lost/Damaged Report Deadline | Weight Limit | Max Cover (RM) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **J&T Express** | Within 24 working hours | Up to 2kg | **200 per shipment** |
| Flash Express | Within 24 hours | All | 200 |
| City-Link | Within 48 working hours | All | 200 |
| Ninja Van | Within 2 working days | All | 200 |
| Lalamove | Within 7 working days | Up to 800kg | 400 |
| Pandago | Within 7 working days | Up to 15kg | 300 |

### 2. InsurePlus (EasyParcel add-on)
- Extra RM50 coverage for lost domestic parcels
- Small add-on fee at booking

### 3. EasyCover (Premium — paid at booking)
- **Cost**: 2%–2.5% of declared parcel value
- **Coverage**: Up to RM10,000
- **Must buy at booking time** — cannot add after parcel is shipped

## Claim Situations

### Situation 1: Lost in Transit / No Update ≥14 Days
**Applies to:** Pos Laju, Skynet, ABX Express, DHL eCommerce, UTS, Ultimate Consolidators, Aramex Domestic

1. Login → Manage Parcels → All Parcel
2. Click 3-line icon → **Report**
3. Select **"Parcel Lost"**
4. Upload: item invoice, photo of content, photo of packaging
5. Track via View Parcel Report

### Situation 2: Delivered but Not Received / Missing Item / Wrong Content
**Applies to:** Parcel marked "Arrived/Delivered" or any courier not in Situation 1

1. Login → Support (sidebar) → **Contact Us**
2. Select **Domestic Shipment** (or International, match your booking)
3. Dropdown → **"Parcel Damaged"** (closest option; no "stolen" option exists)
4. Fill form. Upload:
   - Commercial invoice / item value proof
   - Photo of parcel packaging (POD screenshot showing doorstep)
   - Photo of parcel content (if available)
5. Submit → reply within 24 working hours (Mon–Fri, 10am–6pm, exc. PH)
6. Track via: **Support → Customer Support → Message Board**

### Scenario 2a: Customer Told Courier "Nobody Home" — Parcel Still Dumped

**Stronger negligence case.** Proven in session 2026-07-28: customer explicitly told EasyParcel/J&T chatbot "Saya xde sapa kat rumah pun" (I have nobody at home), but the courier still left the parcel at the doorstep.

**Why this is stronger:**
| Element | Standard case | This scenario |
|---|---|---|
| Courier knowledge | Unknown if anyone was home | ✅ Customer explicitly said nobody home |
| POD evidence | Parcel at doorstep, no handover | ✅ Same |
| Written proof | WhatsApp after delivery | ✅ Chatbot conversation BEFORE delivery |
| Negligence level | "Failed to obtain signature" | "Deliberately ignored customer's instruction + failed to obtain signature" |

**Evidence to keep:**
1. Screenshot of chatbot conversation where customer selected "Saya xde sapa kat rumah pun"
2. POD screenshot (parcel on floor, no handover)
3. Tracking screenshot showing "Arrived" status
4. Invoice/value proof of item

**Argument for EasyParcel support:**
> "My customer explicitly told your courier via the app's chatbot that nobody was home. Despite this, the courier left my RM1,000 parcel at the doorstep unattended. The parcel was stolen. This is not 'delivered' — it's courier negligence. Your own system proves the customer was not home. I demand compensation for the full value."

## Key Deadlines

| Event | Deadline |
|---|---|
| J&T Basic Insurance report | 24 working hours from delivery |
| EasyParcel claim window (Situation 1) | 15th–25th day from pickup date |
| EasyParcel support reply | 24 working hours (weekdays) |
| TTPM hearing | 4–8 weeks after filing |

## Argument Strategy

**Weak framing:** "My parcel is lost, claim insurance"
→ EasyParcel auto-closes: "Parcel delivered. Case closed."

**Strong framing:** "Courier failed to deliver properly. No signature obtained. Parcel left at doorstep without consent. Item stolen due to courier negligence."
→ Escalates to human team. POD photo becomes EVIDENCE AGAINST the courier (not proof for them).

## TTPM Backup

If EasyParcel + seller reject:
- File Borang 1 at TTPM (RM5)
- Claim against seller (not courier) — contract of sale exists with seller
- Amount: RM1,000 (well under RM50,000 cap)
- Bring: invoice, POD, tracking, WhatsApp screenshots
- See `references/ttpm-filing-checklist.md` for full steps
