# BANGANG Profiling — Worked Examples

## Example 1: Anwar Ibrahim (`anwar-jung-shadow`, 2026-07-28)

**Target:** Anwar Ibrahim — reformasi hero persona vs political shadow

**Career gap:** From reformasi martyr to PM who appointed Zahid Hamidi (47 corruption charges) as TPM

**Persona quote:** "Madani," "reformasi," "keadilan untuk semua"

**Shadow action:** 
- Appointed Zahid Hamidi as TPM (first major act as PM)
- Zahid's 47 corruption cases dropped (DNAA Sept 2023) — Anwar silent
- Cabinet filled with UMNO figures he fought for 24 years
- Ringgit at record lows (RM4.70+), SST raised 6%→8%
- Continued same political system he claimed to dismantle

**Human cost:** Rakyat pay more for everything while reformasi narrative continues

**Psychological frame:** Jung's Shadow — the gap between Anwar's reformasi persona and his actual establishment politics. The victim who became what he fought.

**Mirror:** "Jangan tipu diri sendiri — pemilih pun ada bayang."

## Example 2: Ruslan Islahudin (`bangang-ruslan-hr`, 2026-07-30)

**Target:** Ruslan Halim Islahudin — VP HR PETRONAS

**Career gap:** Engineer (Imperial College London) + Master Ekonomi (UM) → never studied HR, never handled workers directly → became SVP & Group CHRO via Risk Management → Lubricants → Leadership Centre

**Persona quote:** "Just transition on three fronts," "resilience isn't a soft skill," "workforce fit for energy transition"

**Shadow action:**
- Led 5,000-worker layoff (10% workforce, 2025)
- Response was a press statement to The Malaysian Reserve, not to workers
- "Transition Council" and "Human Capital Development Committee" formed AFTER layoffs (reactive)
- All public statements are corporate ESG speak — zero human language

**Human cost:**
- Union (Kapenas Sarawak) called for "utmost fairness" — diplomatic for "unfair"
- Reddit: "didn't even have an HR department to complain about abuses"
- "7am-10pm sweatshop, 60-70% of market pay"
- "Salary increment denied because 'true feedback' was marked down by line manager"

**Psychological frame:** INTJ engineer sees humans as headcount → cost to optimize → layoff = "just transition." No step for empathy. The system rewards system-players.

**Mirror:** Arif: "Aku nampak diri aku dalam dia. INTJ macam aku. Tapi beza aku dengan dia — aku nampak shadow aku. Dia tak nampak."

**Mid-session correction (2026-07-30):** Arif rejected naming himself or Abang Sado in the article body. Quote: "Jangan mention nama aku. Dah la mention Abang sado plak. Riuh satu kampung nanti. Make it relatable to all Petronas staff. Faham derita depa. Geng lanyard."

**Fixes applied:**
- `Arif — kawan aku — cakap` → `Ada orang pernah cakap`
- `"Ruslan tu INTJ macam aku. Kalau aku jadi HR, kawan aku baling batu."` → `"Ruslan tu INTJ tulen. Engineer yang tak pernah dilatih handle manusia."`
- `Arif nampak shadow dia dalam Ruslan` → `Ramai yang nampak shadow macam ni dalam Ruslan`
- `Abang Sado — kawan Arif — jual nasi lemak` → `Pemandu lori Petronas, kerani office, technician platform — depa yang rasa.`

**Lesson:** BANGANG profiling of a named person must NOT name the article's author or their associates in the body. Replace named individuals with archetypes the affected community recognizes. The article belongs to the READER's experience, not the author's network.
