# WhatsApp Templates — PRN / Election Campaign (BM)

> **Purpose:** Ready-to-copy WhatsApp messages for makcik/pakcik voters.
> **Style:** Pure spoken BM. No AI language. No analysis. Like real neighbours chatting.
> **When to use:** Arif says "buat template WhatsApp" or "bagi makcik pakcik forward"
> **Source:** 2026-07-28 NS PRN playbook — verified by Arif for tone and cut-through.

---

## Variant 1 — Makcik Santai (Assalamualaikum opener)

Best for: Group jiran, group surau, group pasar. Morning/lunch time.

```
Assalamualaikum.. 🌅

Sabtu 1 Ogos kita undi tau. Jangan lupa.

Bagi pengalaman Makcik la. Dulu masa BN dulu, ok ja. Jalan ok, air ok, barang murah. Tapi lepas PH jadi — makin lama makin susah. Harga naik, air putus, kerja pun susah nak cari.

Kali ni Makcik undi BN-PN. Cuba balik. Takkan lagi teruk dari PH kot? 😅

Yang penting: jangan bagi DAP menang. Depa cuma pandai janji. Cuba tengok sendiri — 8 tahun, apa depa buat untuk kampung kita?

So Makcik pesan — Sabtu ni, pi ngundi. Bawa IC. Janji undi BN atau PN. Dua tu sama ja. Asal jangan DAP.

Share kan kat group jiran okay. Jangan siman sorang.
```

## Variant 2 — Pak Mail (Group lepas subuh)

```
BN dulu 63 tahun perintah. Depa ada banyak salah. Tapi masa depa, ekonomi ok. Kita boleh hidup.

Kali ni saya undi BN. Bukan sebab saya suka UMNO. Tapi sebab saya nak cuba lain. PH dah cuba, tak jadi. Cuba BN balik.

Jangan dengar depa cakap "BN-PN gaduh." Depa dah bagi jalan — BN 25 kerusi, PN 11 kerusi. Dah setel. Tak gaduh. Yang gaduh tu PH sebab depa takut kalah.

Sabtu ni. Bawa IC. Undi BN atau PN. Janji jangan DAP. Salam. 🤝
```

## Rules for all templates

1. **No English paragraphs** — only individual English words that are natural in BM (ok, still, share, group)
2. **No analysis** — not an explainer. It's a nudge.
3. **No links** — templates must work without clicking anything
4. **Short lines** — each paragraph max 3-4 lines on phone screen
5. **One emotion per template** — fear (harga naik), nostalgia (dulu BN ok), or urgency (jangan lupa undi)
6. **No AI framing** — if it reads like a carefully constructed argument, cut 60%
7. **Conversation starter, not lecture** — should feel like what a makcik forwards after morning coffee
