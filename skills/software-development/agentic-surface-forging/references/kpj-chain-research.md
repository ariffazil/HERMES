## Medical eVISA Process
1. Contact KPJ hospital → Appointment letter
2. Hospital provides MHTC support letter
3. Apply online: eVISA Malaysia Immigration (3-5 working days)
4. Required: passport (6mo+), appointment letter, support letter, return ticket, accommodation, photo
5. Special Lane at KLIA, Penang, Johor, Kuching for medical travellers
